About this library

This site is the companion to Najla Elmachtoub's LeadDev workshop “Getting back to flow in human–agent teams.” It collects where flow gets stuck in teams of people and AI agents, the root causes, and People, Process and Tech remediations. Najla curated the starting entries. Everyone can add to it.

Contributions are anonymous and public

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name or email.
  • What you submit appears on the site for everyone straight away, marked “Community.”
  • Please don't include personal data, names of people or companies, confidential information, or anything you don't have the right to share.
  • Najla may edit, hide or remove any contribution at any time, for example spam or off-topic content.

Privacy

  • We don't collect personal data, set our own cookies, or use analytics.
  • Spam protection uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which may set its own cookies and process technical data such as your IP address to tell people from bots. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • To limit abuse, the submission service counts recent submissions using a one-way hash of your connection for up to 10 minutes, in memory only. Your IP address is never stored in our database.

Because contributions are anonymous, we usually can't tell which entry is yours. If you think you've submitted personal data by mistake, email nelmachtoub@gmail.com with the text and roughly when you added it, and it will be removed.
